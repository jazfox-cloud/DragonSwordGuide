# DragonSword Guide design system

## Direction

DragonSword Guide is a dark, information-dense fan guide for readers who want a fast, trustworthy answer about DragonSword: Awakening. The visual world is a night-time Orbis fieldbook: deep navy surfaces, icy cyan signals, silver rules, and compact editorial panels. The homepage persuades visitors to choose a guide; inner pages prioritize reading, evidence, and wayfinding.

## Tokens

- Background: `hsl(222 32% 8%)`
- Surface: `hsl(221 27% 12%)`
- Raised surface: `hsl(219 24% 16%)`
- Foreground: `hsl(210 30% 96%)`
- Muted: `hsl(215 16% 68%)`
- Signal: `hsl(199 86% 58%)`
- Signal light: `hsl(192 92% 68%)`
- Border: `hsl(217 20% 22%)`

## Component language

Use semantic page sections, visible verification labels, restrained rounded panels, 1px signal rules, and 44px-or-larger primary controls. Avoid fake character art, guessed statistics, generic card grids, heavy glow, and official-brand imitation.
